# Contoso Retail — Returns, Refunds & Remedies Policy
Version 4.2 · Effective 01 January 2026 · Owner: Customer Operations

## 1. Scope
1.1 Applies to all consumer purchases made through Contoso Retail online and in-store.
1.2 Does not apply to B2B/trade accounts, which follow the Trade Terms (separate document).

## 2. Standard return window
2.1 Customers may return an item within **30 calendar days of delivery** for a full refund.
2.2 Between 31 and 60 days, only an exchange or store credit is available.
2.3 After 60 days, no remedy is available unless the item is faulty (see section 4).

## 3. Damaged on arrival
3.1 Items reported damaged within **14 days of delivery** are eligible for a **full refund or free replacement**, at the customer's choice. No return shipping cost to the customer.
3.2 Photographic evidence may be requested but must not delay a remedy under $100.
3.3 Items reported damaged after 14 days fall under section 4.

## 4. Faulty items
4.1 Items that develop a fault within **12 months** are eligible for repair or replacement.
4.2 Within the first 6 months, the customer may choose a refund instead.
4.3 Faults caused by misuse, accidental damage, or unauthorised repair are excluded.

## 5. Approval thresholds
5.1 Any refund **exceeding $200** requires manager approval before it is issued.
5.2 Any case categorised as **fraud or suspected fraud** requires manager approval regardless of value.
5.3 Any third refund to the same customer within a rolling 12-month period requires manager approval.

## 6. Wrong or missing items
6.1 Wrong item shipped: full refund or correct item dispatched free, customer's choice, within 30 days.
6.2 Missing item from a multi-item order: dispatch the missing item, or refund that line, within 30 days.

## 7. Late delivery
7.1 Delivery more than 5 days beyond the promised date entitles the customer to a refund of the delivery charge.
7.2 Delivery more than 14 days late entitles the customer to cancel for a full refund.
7.3 Delays caused by extreme weather or declared courier incidents are handled under 7.1 only.

## 8. Exclusions
8.1 Perishable goods, personalised items, and opened hygiene products are non-returnable unless faulty.
8.2 Gift cards are non-refundable.
8.3 Clearance items marked "final sale" are exchange-only within 14 days.

## 9. Refund method and timing
9.1 Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of approval.
9.2 Where the original method is unavailable, store credit is issued.

## 10. Escalation
10.1 Cases the agent cannot resolve under this policy must be escalated to a human manager with a written rationale.
